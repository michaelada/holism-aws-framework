# Merchandise Module Help

## Managing Merchandise

The Merchandise module lets you create and sell products through your organisation.

## Merchandise Types

Define different product categories:
- Apparel (jerseys, t-shirts, hats)
- Equipment and gear
- Accessories and memorabilia
- Digital products

Each type can have:
- Pricing and variants
- Stock management
- Images and descriptions
- Handling fees

## Orders

Track and manage all merchandise orders:
- View order details and status
- Process fulfilment
- Handle returns and refunds
- Export order data

## Discounts

Create promotional discounts for merchandise:
- Percentage or fixed amount off
- Discount codes
- Time-limited offers
- Member-exclusive pricing
